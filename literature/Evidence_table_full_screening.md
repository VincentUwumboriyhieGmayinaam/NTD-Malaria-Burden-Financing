Prepared 21 October 2026. Source: PubMed (via the PubMed MCP server). Grey literature was checked on WHO and IHME web pages. Every number below comes from an abstract or web page that was actually retrieved. DOIs are from PubMed metadata.

Notes on the searches:
- **S4 date filter.** With `date_from=2024` alone, the tool did not apply the date limit and returned 351 records from all years. That is the "~351" expected in the brief. When the window was set explicitly to 2024/01/01–2026/12/31 (publication date), the search returned **99** records. The 99 are counted above.
- **Supplementary targeted searches**, not counted in the totals above:
  - (i) Lancet, Lancet Global Health or Lancet Infectious Diseases AND aid/USAID cuts, 2025–26: 56 records, of which 42 were new and screened by title. This search found the Lancet comments listed in section 3b.
  - (ii) NTD AND (USAID OR funding cuts OR donor funding), 2025–26: 8 records, 3 new. None was a primary projection study. One commentary is noted in 3b.
- **Landmark single-country studies** included and flagged (2 of a maximum of 3): Martins-Melo 2018 (Brazil) and Dolan 2019 (DRC).

## 3. What is missing in the literature (and what our study adds)

1. **No study combines spatial clustering, SDI-based inequality and financing dependence for NTDs and malaria.**
   - GBD-based inequality studies use the concentration index and slope index of inequality by SDI, but none uses Moran's I or LISA, and none includes financing (Feng 2025; Lin 2025; Zhao 2025; Emadi 2021; Stolk 2016).
   - The only spatial-econometric financing study is Namubiru 2025. It covers malaria only, in 38 sub-Saharan African countries, using WHO/MAP case and death rates rather than GBD DALYs.
   - Geostatistical NTD studies model the prevalence of single diseases (Khaki 2025; Schmidt 2022).
2. **No GBD 2023-based analysis of aggregate NTD and malaria DALYs exists in the retrieved set.**
   - GBD 2023 work is limited to the capstone papers (all causes), Chagas disease alone (GBD 2023 Chagas Collaborators 2025), visceral leishmaniasis in the G20 (Wang 2026, PMID 42748112, screened at title stage), and a deaths-only study of 5 BRICS countries (Yin 2026).
   - All 204-country NTD-plus-malaria trend and inequality studies use GBD 2019 or GBD 2021 (Zheng 2025 ×2; Chen 2025; Tuergan 2025; Feng 2025; Lin 2025).
3. **No study uses Theil decomposition** to separate between-region from within-region inequality in NTD and malaria burden. None of the 76 eligible records reports a Theil index.
4. **No within-country panel separates between-country from within-country associations of financing.**
   - Existing panels use pooled or two-way fixed-effects models of DAH or ODA levels against mortality: Bendavid 2014; Hsiao 2014; Yan 2015; Sempungu 2023; Cavalcanti 2025; da Silva 2026.
   - None models the *share* of health spending that is external (dependence), and none uses a within–between (Mundlak or hybrid) specification.
   - Several studies show reverse causality or targeting: DAH is positively associated with burden in Kim 2022 and Namubiru 2025. This motivates within-country estimation.
5. **The outcome is mortality, not DALYs.**
   - The USAID and ODA studies (Cavalcanti 2025; da Silva 2026) estimate NTD and malaria *mortality* risk ratios only.
   - For NTDs, most burden is YLD (disability). No study links financing to NTD DALYs or YLDs.
6. **No country-level, NTD-specific projections of DALYs under the 2025 cuts.**
   - Projections of the cuts cover all-cause or under-5 deaths (Cavalcanti 2025; da Silva 2026), HIV, TB, family planning and maternal and child health (Stover 2025), malaria in one year for PMI only (Symons 2025), and HIV, TB and malaria investment cases (Hallett 2025).
   - The supplementary PubMed search for 2025–26 (NTD AND cuts) found no primary projection study, only commentaries and Downs 2025, which forecasts MDA *needs* rather than health losses.
7. **The evidence on domestic replacement is dated and does not cover NTDs.**
   - Displacement evidence covers HIV, TB and malaria (Lordan 2011; Patenaude 2021) and malaria-eliminating countries (Shretta 2017).
   - No study quantifies whether governments replaced lost external NTD or malaria funding after 2018. WHO reports a 41% fall in NTD ODA between 2018 and 2023 (section 4).

## 4. Grey literature verified (web pages retrieved 21 Oct 2026)

**WHO Global report on neglected tropical diseases 2025** (released 22 Oct 2025). Sources: [WHO news release](https://www.who.int/news/item/22-10-2025-global-progress-against-neglected-tropical-diseases-continues-despite-challenges--new-who-report-finds) and [report page](https://www.who.int/teams/control-of-neglected-tropical-diseases/global-report-on-neglected-tropical-diseases-2025).
- People requiring NTD interventions in 2023: **1.495 billion**, 122 million fewer than in 2022. This is a **32% decrease from the 2010 baseline**.
- In 2023, 867.1 million people were treated for at least one NTD.
- NTD DALYs fell from 17.2 million to 14.1 million between 2015 and 2021, and NTD deaths fell from about 139,000 to 119,000.
- Official development assistance for NTDs **fell by 41% between 2018 and 2023**.
- Countries eliminating NTDs:
  - The WHO pages state that **7 countries** were acknowledged in 2024 for eliminating an NTD.
  - The cumulative figure of **54 countries eliminating at least one NTD by 2024** comes from a secondary summary of the report ([zeroleprosy.org](https://zeroleprosy.org/?p=2858)). I could not see it on the WHO pages themselves.

**World Malaria Report 2025** (released 4 Dec 2025). Source: [WHO news release](https://www.who.int/news/item/04-12-2025-new-tools-saved-a-million-lives-from-malaria-last-year-but-progress-under-threat-as-drug-resistance-rises); report page [here](https://www.who.int/teams/global-malaria-programme/reports/world-malaria-report-2025).
- **282 million cases** in 2024, about 9 million more than in 2023.
- **610,000 deaths**, a mortality rate of 13.8 per 100,000, more than three times the global target of 4.5.
- About 95% of deaths were in the WHO African Region.
- New tools prevented an estimated 170 million cases and 1 million deaths in 2024.
- Funding in 2024 was US$3.9 billion, against a 2025 target of US$9.3 billion.
- WHO states that ODA reductions "severely disrupted health systems".

**IHME Financing Global Health 2025.**
- Peer-reviewed version: Apeagyei et al., *Lancet* 2025 (in the table). DAH was US$80.3 billion in 2021, US$49.6 billion in 2024, an estimated US$38.4 billion in 2025, and a forecast US$36.2 billion in 2030.
- The press summary of the report ([EATG](https://www.eatg.org/hiv-news/funding-for-lifesaving-global-health-programs-forecasted-to-reach-15-year-low-threatening-to-reverse-decades-of-progress/)) gives rounded figures:
  - about US$39 billion in 2025, a 51% drop from the US$80 billion peak and the lowest in 15 years;
  - US$36 billion forecast for 2030;
  - the USA made the largest reduction among donors (−67%);
  - sub-Saharan Africa saw an estimated 25% fall from the previous year.
- No malaria-specific or NTD-specific DAH figures were reported on the pages retrieved.
